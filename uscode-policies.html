Refund policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Every US CODE tee is printed to order and personalized for you, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or the wrong size. Please check the size guide before you order. If anything is wrong with your piece, we'll make it right.

Damaged, misprinted or faulty items

If your tee arrives damaged, misprinted, or with an NFC chip that doesn't work, email us at support@theuscode.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. Once confirmed, we'll reprint and ship a replacement at no cost, or issue a full refund if you prefer.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order for a full refund within 12 hours of placing it. After that, your piece has gone into production and can no longer be cancelled.

Personalization errors

We print exactly the photo and details you submit. We can't refund or reprint for errors in images or information you provided, so please review your upload carefully. If we made the mistake, we'll reprint for free.

Your memory

The photo, video, voice note or song linked to your chip can be changed at any time, so there's no need to return a tee to update its memory.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Depending on your bank, it may take a little longer to appear.

Contact

Questions? Email support@theuscode.com.

Shipping policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Production time

Each US CODE tee is made to order. After you place your order and submit any personalization, production takes 5–7 business days before your order ships.

Delivery

  • United Arab Emirates: free delivery, typically 2–4 business days after dispatch.
  • International: we ship worldwide. Rates are calculated at checkout based on your address, and delivery typically takes 7–14 business days after dispatch.

Delivery times are estimates and may vary because of carrier delays, customs or public holidays.

Tracking

You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Customs and duties

International orders may be subject to import duties and taxes, which are set by the destination country and paid by the customer. We have no control over these charges.

Address accuracy

Please make sure your shipping address is correct. We're not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us because of an incorrect address, we'll reship it once you cover the extra shipping cost.

Lost or damaged in transit

If your parcel is lost or arrives damaged, email support@theuscode.com within 14 days of the expected delivery date and we'll help resolve it with the carrier.
